      How to play

      1. Read the case. A fly's brain was damaged. When the stimulus arrives it no longer behaves like a healthy fly.
      2. Open the circuit map. Every dot is a real cell type from the male fruit-fly connectome. Lines are real synapses.
      3. Operate. Click a dot to Boost (stimulate) or Silence it. You can't touch sensors or the command neurons; you can only rewire the middle.
      4. Restore the behaviour to the healthy level without waking up the wrong behaviours (collateral damage costs points).
      5. Every fix you save becomes a candidate circuit in your case file that you can export for researchers.

      The simulator is a rate model running on the real wiring of a pathway subgraph. It is a teaching and exploration tool, not a prediction of living-fly behaviour.
      Wiring: FlyEM male-CNS connectome v1.0 (via the ref fly-brain project). Built for the ML Empowerment Build Challenge 3.0.
