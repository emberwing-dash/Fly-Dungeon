How it works

Every fly runs a live copy of a slice of the real fruit-fly connectome: 6,484 neurons and 193,169 connections taken from the FlyEM male-CNS wiring diagram, through the open Lulzx/fly-brain project.

  • An enemy growing in view drives the shadow detectors (LC4, LPLC2), which fire the escape neurons. The fly dashes away.
  • The smell of the key and the exit drives the approach neurons. The fly pushes toward it.
  • Honey touches the taste cells. The proboscis neuron must fire for the fly to eat.

The wiring does not say how strong synapses are or when to act. Those 8 numbers per fly are evolved: when a fly dies, the next one hatches from the best brains so far, slightly mutated.

Where to go (the path) is supplied by the game, as in other embodied fly simulators. The brain decides whether to flee, push on, or eat.
